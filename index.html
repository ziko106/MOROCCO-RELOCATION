🛂 Carte de Séjour (Residency) & Banking

• 90-Day Entry: US, UK, EU, Canada & GCC citizens enter visa-free for 90 days.

• Carte de Séjour Requirements: Lease contract (Bail légalisé), Moroccan bank account proof (Attestation de solde), criminal record check, medical certificate & photos.

• Convertible Dirham Account: Foreigners & MREs must open a Compte en Dirhams Convertibles to freely repatriate funds or rental income abroad.

💼 Moroccan Business & Tax Structures

💻 Auto-Entrepreneur (Services):1% Turnover Tax (Up to 200k MAD/yr)
🏢 SARL (Limited Company):20% Corporate Tax (Standard SME)
🌐 Casablanca Finance City (CFC):Preferential Tax & FX Status
💡 183-Day Rule: Spending 183+ days/year in Morocco makes you a tax resident; check double-taxation treaties (US, France, UK, Canada, Spain, UAE).

🔑 Safe Property Buying Protocol (Notaire)

1. Titre Foncier Only: Never buy untitled land (*Melkia*) as a foreign buyer—insist on a registered Titre Foncier at the ANCFCC.

2. Closing Costs (~7%–8%): 4% Registration Duty + 1.5% Land Conservation + ~1% Notaire Fees + VAT.

3. Office des Changes Guarantee: Wire purchase funds from abroad directly to the Notaire's escrow account (*CDG*) to guarantee 100% capital & capital-gains repatriation later.
